About us

Our Mission

India Heritage Initiative takes leaders and individuals from diverse backgrounds to India on educational and exploratory visits. We believe that seeing a place, meeting its people and learning its history in person creates a deeper understanding than any book or briefing can.

Our participants include faith leaders, interfaith leaders, political leaders and their staff, and community leaders of all backgrounds. Each journey is designed to be thoughtful, respectful and open to every perspective.

  • Learn — history, culture and traditions, firsthand.
  • Connect — with people, communities and one another.
  • Share — understanding that travels home.
